Where do I find my token?

Run this on the Docker VM:

docker exec torrent-intake cat /app/data/admin-token

Use HTTPS or a trusted localhost tunnel. The token is kept only in this page's memory, not browser storage. A fresh installation has its own token, including after restore.

Pausing stops Intake's management and scan workers, not qBittorrent downloads or moves already requested. Wait for “drained” before saving or backing up.

Saved, awaiting restart

The running process still has its old settings. In Portainer, restart torrent-intake. Reopen this page, unlock administration, then choose Verify & resume. No Docker socket or automatic restart is used.
